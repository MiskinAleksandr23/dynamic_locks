simple genetic partition demo
  requests: sum on range + apply f(int)->int to one element
  chromosome: partition boundaries between 1024 fine-grained blocks
  mutation: shift a few boundaries by several blocks
  fitness = contention_score + 5000 * avg_mutexes_per_query
  contention_score = sum(load_i^2), load_i = recent queries on mutex i
  online learning: batches are clustered into workload modes,
  each mode keeps its own history and remembered best partition
